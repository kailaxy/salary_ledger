## Contract

Operations.gs is a deterministic, network-free planner shared verbatim by Apps Script and the hosted/bundled frontend. `python3 tools/embed-operations.py` updates its marked block in Index.html after source changes; a test checks exact parity. Server dispatch accepts an authenticated, allowlisted `syncOperation` envelope, never arbitrary rows/functions/code. The planner validates fields, enums, cents, dates, references, duplicate names, schedules, fund balances and archive/deletion constraints before preparing changes. A loan and all installments, a category and fund goal, or a payment and mirrored spending are a single recoverable bundle.

A SHA-256 context covers normalized business fields and tombstone state in the relevant tables. It ignores created/updated audit timestamps and physical row order, so local dependent changes can match server acknowledgements despite different clocks. All material direct Sheet changes in those tables cause a conflict. Contexts are deliberately conservative rather than minimal per-field locks. Permanent generated IDs are derived from the full mutation identity and collision-checked. The server rebuilds the proposed effects itself. It does not trust client-supplied financial rows. Bounds: at most 150 changed rows and 45,000 characters of prepared effects; oversized changes are rejected before confirmation commits and can be split by the owner.

PREPARED journal intent includes original and exact intended rows, then additions and contiguous existing rows are written in batches, flushed, and every effect is verified before the durable COMMITTED receipt. Replaying equal intended rows does not duplicate them. Before application reads/writes, the script lock recovers pending bundles. Recovery reconstructs the before context to detect unrelated material changes. Untouched intent can conflict; once any target differs from its original value, an unexpected target or related direct Sheet edit stops recovery rather than abandoning a half-applied bundle. Preserve the journal and restore its recorded before/after values before retrying. LockService cannot serialize a human editing cells at the same moment; app-mediated writes remain preferable. Do not erase the journal or clear device data to bypass recovery.

## Device queue

Confirmation atomically stores the operation, full proposed effects and local audit in IndexedDB. Projections recompute all screens from complete tables; pending overlays never replace the saved cloud base. Dependencies keep changes using newly created categories/loans behind their prerequisites. Ready ordinary edits/new-loan payments can rebase only audit fingerprints, after verifying their confirmed business state matches the acknowledged base. Payloads are durably marked attempted before transport and never rewritten on retry, including after a crash or lost reply. Complete operation receipts are checked against the confirmed effects and applied together with queue removal in one IndexedDB transaction. Partial or changed receipts leave the queue intact.

A conflict preserves its exact proposal and uses cloud values for dependent projections. Discard requires explicit review of freshly fetched cloud data and a second context check. Discarding a prerequisite marks its descendants for review, rather than leaving them blocked forever or applying against missing records. The conservative dependency chain may delay unrelated changes. A single loan still accepts one pending new payment at a time; editing that pending payment can be queued after it.
